Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Ramencraft does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your favorite recipes and journal entries (ratings and notes) are stored locally on your iPhone. We cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Ramencraft schedules local alerts when a timer finishes. These are created on your device and no data is sent anywhere.

No tracking

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the support page.
